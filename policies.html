Privacy & shop information

Updated October 21, 2026. Store preview: no orders or payments are accepted.

Privacy for this preview

Design selections and personalization text are saved only in this browser using localStorage. They are not sent to us, a supplier, or a payment processor. Clear them using Saved → Clear saved selections, or delete this site's browser data. Avoid entering sensitive information. We use no analytics scripts, advertising cookies, third-party fonts or contact-form processors.

GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process network data for hosting and security under GitHub's privacy statement. A future external checkout has its own privacy policy. We do not collect card details here.

Draft policies — pending owner approval

These describe the proposed workflow, not promises of active service. Final listing terms, merchant contact details and supplier terms must be approved before ordering opens.

Production & shipping draft

Each listing must name the exact size, material, finish, production partner, destination availability, production estimate, shipping price and delivery estimate. Production and transit are separate; no guaranteed arrival dates are offered. Tracking, where supplied, will be shared through the marketplace. Delays and address corrections require review before production.

Personalization approval draft

Buyers must confirm exact spelling, dates, punctuation and selected options in the marketplace. Preview text on this website does not transfer. Hold personalized orders for manual proof review. Request buyer approval through marketplace messages before releasing any order needing a proof. No response means hold, not automatic approval.

Cancellations, damage & returns draft

Review cancellation requests before production and confirm supplier cancellation status before promising a refund. Proposed handling: investigate damage, defects and incorrect items with photos and order details through the marketplace. Supplier reimbursement rules do not replace buyer rights. Personalized and digital products need explicit listing-specific change-of-mind terms that comply with applicable requirements. No deadline or blanket exclusion is active yet.

Contact

A verified business support address and monitored contact channel must be published before launch. No message submission service is connected; this page does not collect inquiries.

Analytics

Analytics is off. If optional analytics is introduced, add a prior opt-in, a decline option and a way to withdraw consent before loading it.
