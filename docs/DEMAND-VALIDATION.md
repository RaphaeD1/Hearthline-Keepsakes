## Cost and pricing gate

Proposed format: one unframed 8 × 10 inch print, US destinations first. Blueprint 282 publicly lists 8 × 10 among its sizes and supports personalization; actual provider variant and shipping eligibility must be confirmed in the owner's account. Public starting production price is $4.96 and starting shipping is $5.19. These are separate “from” amounts, NOT a combined confirmed 8 × 10 quote. Do not promise its advertised average production time as a delivery deadline.

Planned pilot price: $29.95 with buyer shipping included. This is a price hypothesis, not a verified market-clearing price. A competitor's opened new-home listing displayed an 8 × 10 option at $13.92 before destination shipping, so premium positioning needs validation. Final listing price must follow an exact cost quote and the actual channel fee schedule.

US Etsy scenario: 6.5% transaction + 3% processing, $0.25 processing fixed + $0.20 listing allocation, 5% refund reserve, $3 labor per order (10 minutes at $18/hour assumption). Sales-tax processing effects, currency conversion, overhead and other account-specific charges are excluded and must be added where applicable. A separate 15% attributed-ad scenario is stress-tested; actual applicable rate must be confirmed. Do not use this fee model for Printify Pop-Up.

Proposed go/no-go rule: at least $8 contribution per order after labor/reserve before overhead, plus positive stressed contribution. At $29.95 this allows at most $14.15725 delivered supplier cost under base fees, or $9.66475 if the same $8 target must survive a 15% ad fee. Use unrounded values for decisions. An unverified quote cannot pass. Increase price or change product/channel if actual landed cost fails; never count gross revenue as profit.

## What is still needed to call something a winner

An authorized Etsy seller session is needed to read actual Marketplace Insights query demand/competition; the link currently resolves to sign-in. No search-volume number or keyword “low competition” claim is verified. Suggested queries: personalized family name print, surname print, anniversary date print, first home print, housewarming print. Record available period, geography, searches and competing listings; do not combine incompatible periods or treat search/listing ratios as guaranteed conversion.

After compliant listings and fulfillment are approved, run an organic pilot with no paid ads authorized. Suggested internal test rules, not industry benchmarks: evaluate each design after 100 qualified listing visits or 30 days; if traffic is below the threshold, demand remains inconclusive. Review price, imagery and search relevance if no paid orders occur. Label a provisional winner only after at least five independent non-test paid orders, positive actual contribution after fees/labor/refunds, approved personalization, and successful delivery; continue monitoring repeatability. No fake reviews, competitor copying, invented scarcity or claimed order counts.
