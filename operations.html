Launch operations

This public checklist contains no customer records, live order feed or sales totals. Use marketplace and supplier dashboards for real orders.

Readiness

  • Store: preview only; checkout disabled.
  • Merchant identity, payouts and support channel: owner action needed.
  • Supplier, SKU, variant, landed cost and destination quote: unverified.
  • Authorized listing URLs, sample quality, buyer policies and test purchase: pending.

US Etsy contribution calculator

Scenario tool, not a supplier quote. Includes 6.5% transaction fee, 3% + $0.25 processing and $0.20 listing allocation. Includes your entered labor cost; excludes tax effects, currency conversion and fixed overhead. Add Offsite Ads when applicable. A 5% refund reserve is included.

Launch rule: at the planned $29.95 retail price, require at least $8 contribution after labor and reserve. A 15% attributed ad fee needs a separate check. Cost inputs above are assumptions until supplier quotes are entered.

Every order

  1. Verify paid/captured status inside the marketplace; never rely on an email or browser redirect.
  2. Match marketplace order ID, supplier order ID, SKU, variant, address, buyer text and proof approval. Keep customer details out of GitHub.
  3. Hold exceptions: missing personalization, invalid address, unavailable variant, duplicate import, payment review or negative margin.
  4. Confirm supplier acceptance and promised dispatch; review overdue orders daily.
  5. Check tracking and delivery; contact buyers through approved marketplace channels.
  6. Review cancellation/refund requests against production status. Log resolution in the merchant system and reconcile fees and payouts weekly.

Never use this page to enter customer information. See repository docs for integration and launch gates.
